  ⚠ Bugs: Missing effect dependencies ×8
    Learn more: https://react.doctor/docs/rules/react-doctor/exhaustive-deps
    `useMemo` can run with a stale `state.currentUser,
    state.currentUser.id, state.currentUser.following` & show

    callback first.

      setCount(count + 1);

    }, []);
